Frequently asked questions

What documents do I need to clear an import?+

Typically: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, and any permits for controlled goods. Importing a vehicle also needs the export certificate and proof of payment. Send us what you have — we'll tell you exactly what's missing.

How long does customs clearance take at Walvis Bay?+

With complete documents, straightforward entries are often released within 24–48 hours of lodging. Inspections or queries can add time — which is exactly why we pre-check every document before submission.

How much duty will I pay?+

Duties depend on the tariff classification and customs value of your goods. As part of every quote, we give you a duty & VAT estimate up front so there are no surprises. Note: duties are set by NamRA, not by us.

Can you move cargo to Zambia, DRC or Botswana?+

Yes — corridor transit is one of our specialities. We arrange bonded transit through the Trans-Kalahari, Trans-Caprivi and Trans-Cunene corridors, including transit bonds and border documentation.

Do you handle private / once-off imports?+

Absolutely. From a single vehicle to household goods, we clear once-off consignments for individuals every week — with the same care we give commercial clients.
